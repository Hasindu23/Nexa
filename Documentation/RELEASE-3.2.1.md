# Nexa 3.2.1 — Wide Electric Blue Visualizer

Build 13 adds a wide, segmented blue visualizer below the Music playback controls, filling the previously empty space. Columns use royal-blue, electric-blue and pale-blue gradients with a faded mirrored reflection.

This is a decorative playback-state animation, not a frequency spectrum or audio analysis. No microphone or audio capture is used. It stops when playback pauses, when hidden/occluded, with Reduce Motion, and in Energy Saving. Energy Saving also removes its reflection and glow. Existing compact Bars/Wave/Minimal controls remain available.

The 3.2 workspace, storage cleanup safety, saved data, permissions and media transport remain unchanged. Validation and screenshots are in `Visualizer-3.2.1-QA`. The app remains locally signed, not Apple-notarized; physical external displays, Intel execution and older macOS runtimes are not validated by this patch.
